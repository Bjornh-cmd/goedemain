Privacy Policy — Offline Games No Wifi

Last updated: August 31, 2026
Developer: FinoviApps
App name: Offline Games No Wifi
Package name: com.FinoviApps.OfflineGames

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how FinoviApps (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the mobile application Offline Games No Wifi (“the App”).

The App is designed to work without Wi‑Fi for gameplay. A small amount of online communication is used only for a one-time first-open notification and optional bug reports.

Information we collect and use

1. Information stored on your device only

The following data is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers:

  • Game high scores
  • App preferences (such as selected theme)
  • A local flag indicating whether the first-open notification has already been sent

This data remains on your device until you reset scores in the App, clear app data, or uninstall the App.

We do not require an account, email address, or phone number to use the App.

2. Information sent over the internet

The App requests internet access for the following limited purposes:

a) First app open (automatic, once)

When you open the App for the first time and an internet connection is available, the App may send a short notification to our developer support channel (via a Discord webhook). This may include:

  • That a first open occurred
  • Basic device operating system information (e.g. Android version text provided by the system)
  • The App package name

This does not include your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

b) Bug reports (only if you choose to send one)

If you use the Report a bug feature, the App sends:

  • The text you type in the bug report
  • The game name you select (if any)
  • Basic device operating system information

Please do not include passwords, personal addresses, ID numbers, or other sensitive information in bug reports.

How we use this information

We use the information above to:

  • Monitor new installs at a high level
  • Investigate and fix bugs
  • Improve the App

We do not:

  • Sell your personal data
  • Use advertising SDKs
  • Use third-party analytics SDKs for marketing profiles
  • Create user accounts or profiles

In-app purchases

Payments for premium content (Pong) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Third-party services

Bug reports and first-open notifications are delivered using Discord webhook infrastructure. Discord may process message content according to its own Privacy Policy.

Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for accounts or marketing. Bug report fields are free text; users should not enter personal information about children.

Data retention

  • On-device data is kept until you reset it or uninstall the App.
  • Webhook messages (first-open and bug reports) may be retained in our developer channel as long as needed for support and improvement, then removed during ordinary channel maintenance.

Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Play games without submitting a bug report
  • Reset local high scores in the App
  • Uninstall the App to remove locally stored data

Without internet access, first-open notifications and bug reports cannot be sent.

Depending on your country, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us using the details below.

Security

Data sent online is transmitted over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
